Privacy Policy

How the VINI workspace application accesses, uses and stores data from a connected Google Account.

Effective 14 September 2026

This policy explains what data the VINI workspace application (the “Application”) reads from a Google Account, why it reads it, where that data goes, and how it is removed. It applies only to Google Accounts that have been explicitly connected to the Application by their owner.

1. Who operates this

The Application is a private, single-user tool operated by Imran Haruna Abdullah, Kano, Nigeria. It is not offered as a public service and has no users other than its operator. Questions about this policy, or requests concerning your data, go to imranaharuna551@gmail.com.

2. What Google data the Application accesses

Once you approve the consent screen, the Application can reach the following, and nothing else:

  • Gmail: read messages, threads and labels; apply and remove labels; create drafts; and send email on your behalf. The requested Gmail scopes are gmail.readonly, gmail.labels, gmail.modify, gmail.compose and gmail.send.
  • Google Sheets: read spreadsheets and their cell contents (spreadsheets.readonly).
  • Google Drive: read-only listing and file metadata (drive.readonly), requested only because the Sheets tools cannot locate a spreadsheet without it. This scope does not grant access to the contents of your files.

Access is confined to the account that granted consent. The Application cannot see, and has no way to reach, any other Google Account. No Calendar, Contacts, Docs, Photos or Drive-write access is requested at any point.

3. How the data is used

For one purpose only: to carry out instructions given by the account owner: for example, finding a message, summarising unread mail, applying a label, drafting or sending a reply, or reading figures out of a spreadsheet.

The data is not used for advertising, is not used to build profiles, is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending decisions, and is not used for any purpose unrelated to the owner's direct instruction.

4. Automated (AI) processing

The Application is a personal AI assistant. To act on an instruction, the content relevant to that instruction is sent to a large language model provider so the request can be understood and answered. That processing happens only to produce the response to the instruction that triggered it. The content is not used to train any model, and is not retained by the provider beyond what is required to return the response.

5. Storage and security

  • Google OAuth credentials are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a local credentials directory, and are protected by that machine's file permissions and account login.
  • No copy of your Google data is held on any server operated by us. No such server exists, the Application runs locally and calls Google's APIs directly.
  • All traffic to and from Google is encrypted in transit with TLS.

6. Sharing and disclosure

  • Google user data is never sold, rented or traded.
  • It is not transferred to third parties for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any purpose not described in this policy.
  • It is not used to determine creditworthiness, and is not used for lending purposes of any kind.
  • No human other than the account owner reads it. There is no human review, and no staff or contractors with access.
  • The only transfers that occur are: to Google's own APIs, to serve the request; transiently to the language model provider described in section 4; and where disclosure is required by law.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

The Application's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Access credentials remain on the operator's computer until access is revoked.
  • You can revoke the Application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once revoked, the stored credential stops working immediately and is deleted from the local credentials directory.
  • To have any other data deleted, or to ask what is held, email imranaharuna551@gmail.com.
  • Email already sent at the owner's instruction cannot be recalled, as with any mail client.

9. Children

The Application is a private tool for its operator. It is not directed at children and is not made available to them.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised if the Application's behaviour changes. The effective date at the top of this page will change whenever it does.
