About This Fan

The Portable Mini Water-Cooling Fan (model DP-7645) pairs a high-power brushless motor with a built-in water tank, so you get real airflow plus a fine cooling mist whenever the heat gets too much.

It's small enough to hold in one hand, but strong enough for your office desk, your car, or your bag on a hot afternoon. Add water, pick your speed, and enjoy cooler air wherever you are — and if you want a scented breeze, you can add a fragrance bottle too (sold separately).

Care Tips

  • 💧 Don't overfill the water tank — fill to the marked line only.
  • 🧴 Wipe the fan dry after use, especially around the mist nozzle.
  • 🔋 Charge fully before first use for the best battery life.
  • 🚫 Never submerge the fan body or motor in water.
